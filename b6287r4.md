## What and why

Fixes #6123. A mod could not draw a fluid on a `GuiCanvas`: the canvas had `fill`, `item`, `atlas_sprite` and the rest, but nothing for a fluid, and no client read answers another mod's fluid sprite and tint, so mods guessed `block/<fluid>_still` with water's tint. The engine already resolves a fluid's still sprite and tint once per load for an item's fluid layer (`fluid_models::item_appearance`); the canvas had no way to use it.

This adds proposal 1 of the issue: `GuiCanvas::fluid(&mut self, fluid: &FluidStack, rect: [f32; 4]) -> Result<(), GuiDrawError>`.
- It draws the engine's one appearance of the fluid, the one an item's fluid layer and the world use (`fluid_models::item_appearance`): a registered fluid model's still sprite, else the look's sprite, else water's; the look's tint made opaque, white for a sprite with no tint, water's item colour 0xFF3F76E4 for a look with neither. Vanilla water is `block/water_still` times 0xFF3F76E4, lava `block/lava_still` untinted (`FluidContentsTint` answers -1 for lava).
- The sprite is read with `HudAtlas::texture_rect`, so a packed block-model sprite is found (the GUI lookup hides those); a registered atlas's sprite binds its page. A fluid no model knows draws the missing sprite untinted, as `FluidStateModelSet.get`'s `missingModel` does for an item; the empty fluid draws nothing.
- The sprite tiles at its own pixel size from the top left of `rect`, the last column and row cut, as JEI's `FluidTankRenderer.drawTiledSprite`. The fill is one quad, six vertices, over the part of `rect` the clip can show (the clip is the canvas scissor, else the screen), whatever `rect`, the pose's scale or the screen's size (any finite `f32` is valid; JEI counts in `int` GUI pixels, vanilla has no GUI fluid draw). The shared HUD shader repeats the sprite across that quad, so no geometry grows with screen area or with the number of repeats. The visible part is the rectangle meeting the clip mapped back through the inverse pose, on the grid still anchored at the rectangle's top left. The repeat phase at the clip edge comes from exact `rem_euclid` remainders, not from an index counted from a distant origin, and corners are posed in `f64` before narrowing to `f32`, so `[-1e30, 0, 2e30, 16]` and a rectangle at 2^29 posed back on screen keep whole repeats. An empty clip, a rectangle outside it, or a singular pose (zero area) adds nothing. Nothing is ever stretched.
- The repeat lives in the shared GPU GUI path (`sunrise-client-render`). `HudBatch::quad_tiled` writes each corner's place on the repeating grid (in repeats of the sprite) to `uv`, the sprite's atlas origin to `glint_uv`, and its atlas extent as two `f16` to `light` (decoded in the shader with integer and `exp2` arithmetic, `half_bits`, since the OpenGL backend's GLSL 330 has no `unpackHalf2x16`). The new `Blend::Tiled` (and `AtlasBlend::Tiled` for a sprite on a registered atlas page) draws those vertices with `hud.wgsl`'s `fs_tiled`. That shader wraps the place into the sprite's sub-rectangle (`origin + fract(place) * extent`) and samples through the HUD's atlas sampler at level 0, the atlas's only mip level, so the jump in the wrapped coordinate at a repeat's edge never selects a level. Each device pixel samples the texel the full tiling puts at its centre, as per-repeat quads would, and the four vertices carry proper, distinct coordinates. The vertex layout is unchanged: `glint_uv` and `light` are already per-vertex attributes, and `light` is `flat`.
- Uses the canvas pose and scissor like `fill`; corners are sorted; a non-finite rect gives `NotFinite`. A fluid that is not registered (an unresolved saved fluid) is refused with the new `GuiDrawError::UnknownFluid`.
- The stack's amount and components do not change the drawing; the engine has no tint provider that reads them yet (documented).

Not done: proposal 2, `ClientHost::fluid_look`. The issue says "either or both"; the canvas call covers AE2's terminal entries and fluid gauges, and a host read needs a new Wire type and dispatch. It can follow separately.

## Layer

Mod API vocab, the client's GUI canvas, and the shared HUD renderer (one fragment entry and pipeline for a repeated atlas sprite); no new dependency.

## Vanilla reference

NeoForge `IClientFluidTypeExtensions.getStillTexture(FluidStack)` and `getTintColor(FluidStack)`, read here through the same resolution as `DynamicFluidContainerModel` / `FluidContentsTint`; JEI's `FluidTankRenderer.drawTiledSprite` tiles the still sprite the same way. Vanilla 26.3 has no GUI fluid draw of its own.
