## What and why

Fixes #6037. A mod structure piece can only write through `PieceContext::set_block`, which is `StructurePiece.placeBlock`: it writes with flags 2, schedules a fluid tick when the position holds a fluid, and marks a few states for post-processing. Applied Energistics 2's meteorite piece writes its crater with `WorldGenLevel.setBlock`, so on Sunrise the crater's water or lava may flow once when the chunk is placed.

What the commit adds:
- `PieceContext::set_block_raw(pos, state) -> bool`: `WorldGenLevel.setBlock(pos, state, 2)`. Same write (flags 2) and same clipping to `chunk_box` as `set_block`, without the two extras `placeBlock` adds: no fluid tick, and no piece-level post-processing mark of a shape-check block (`SHAPE_CHECK_BLOCKS`: torches, fences, ladders, iron bars). The level still applies the block state's own post-processing rule for flags 2 (soul sand still marks the position above it), and the call doesn't cancel a queued tick.
- An unregistered state, a position outside the chunk box and a write failure answer `false`, as for `set_block`.
- `Placing::write(pos, state, raw)` is the shared body. `set_block` calls it with `raw` false, which still goes through `legacy::write_block`.
- `PieceContext` has one implementor (the game), so no other type needs the method.

Tests:
- `native_structure_types::tests::a_raw_write_skips_the_fluid_tick_and_the_mark_that_place_block_adds` (not a daily test). `RecordingWorld` records each write's flags, the scheduled fluid ticks and the positions marked for post-processing; the test registry gains a `minecraft:torch` state as the shape-check block. With water at every position it writes stone and a torch through `set_block_raw` and through `set_block`, and asserts:
  - the flags of all four writes are `2` (a raw path that used `18` would also suppress the level's own mark);
  - only `set_block` schedules fluid ticks (two, for its two positions);
  - only `set_block` marks the torch for post-processing, and neither marks stone;
  - `set_block_raw` is clipped to the chunk box (two outside writes return `false`).
