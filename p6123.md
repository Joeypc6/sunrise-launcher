## What and why

Fixes #6123. A mod could not draw a fluid on a `GuiCanvas`: the canvas had `fill`, `item`, `atlas_sprite` and the rest, but nothing for a fluid, and no client read answers another mod's fluid sprite and tint, so mods guessed `block/<fluid>_still` with water's tint. The engine already resolved a fluid's look for the recipe panel (`Well::new`, `fluid_tint`, `native_fluid`); it was not exposed.

This adds proposal 1 of the issue: `GuiCanvas::fluid(&mut self, fluid: &FluidStack, rect: [f32; 4]) -> Result<(), GuiDrawError>`.
- Draws the sprite the fluid's look (`data/<mod>/sunrise/fluid/<name>.json`) names, tiled at its own pixel size from the top left of `rect`, the right and bottom tiles cut, multiplied by the look's tint. A fluid whose look names no sprite (vanilla water and lava, a registered fluid with no look) draws the block atlas's `<namespace>:block/<path>_still` the same way when there is one, as a recipe viewer draws vanilla fluids: `block/water_still` times water's biome-less default 0xFF3F76E4, `block/lava_still` untinted. With no such sprite it fills `rect` with its colour, as the recipe panel does (the look's tint, else 0xFF3F76E4 water, 0xFFFF6A00 lava, 0xFF9A9A9A).
- Uses the canvas pose and scissor like `fill`; corners are sorted; a non-finite rect gives `NotFinite`.
- A fluid that is not registered (an unresolved saved fluid) is refused with the new `GuiDrawError::UnknownFluid`.
- More than 4096 tiles stretches one sprite instead of looping.
- The stack's amount and components do not change the look; the engine has no tint provider that reads them yet (documented).

Not done: proposal 2, `ClientHost::fluid_look`. The issue says "either or both"; the canvas call covers AE2's terminal entries and fluid gauges, and a host read needs a new Wire type and dispatch. It can follow separately.

## Vanilla reference

NeoForge `IClientFluidTypeExtensions.getStillTexture(FluidStack)` and `getTintColor(FluidStack)`; JEI's `FluidTankRenderer` tiles the still sprite at 16 px and tints it the same way. Vanilla 26.3 has no GUI fluid draw of its own.
