Tests in `chat_tests.rs`: `a_keyed_replacement_that_wraps_to_more_or_fewer_lines_keeps_the_readers_place` (more lines, fewer lines, scrolled up, at the bottom, focused and not, scroll clamp) and `a_keyed_replacement_follows_the_timestamps_and_the_search` (timestamps on with a new time, search hiding and showing the message). `remove_keyed` is unchanged.
