## What and why

Fixes #6126. Nothing lets a mod make a player collect a given item entity or experience orb at once through the game's own pickup. Blood Magic's Sigil of Magnetism, like many magnet items, calls `itemEntity.playerTouch(player)` and `orb.playerTouch(player)` for each one in range. The Blood Magic port moves them onto the player and waits for the next player tick, one tick late. Copying the stack into the inventory by hand would skip the pickup hooks, the statistics, the animation and Mending.

What the commit adds, on `PlayerRef` (server side):
- `touch_item(host, item) -> Result<u32, PickupError>` (backend `player_touch_item`): `ItemEntity.playerTouch` now. It runs the game's pickup with its delay and target checks, `item_picked_up` and `item_picked_up_post`, `Inventory.add` (what does not fit stays on the ground), the statistic, the advancement criterion, and the take animation and sound. `Ok(count)` is what the inventory took, 0 when refused.
- `touch_orb(host, orb) -> Result<bool, PickupError>` (backend `player_touch_orb`): `ExperienceOrb.playerTouch` now. `Ok(false)` while `takeXpDelay` runs. Otherwise the delay is set to 2, one of the orb's count is taken with animation and sound, its value goes through Mending, the rest to the player's experience, and a one-count orb is discarded.
- New `PickupError { Stale, Unavailable, InTransaction }`. `Stale` also for a dead player: the game's pickup pass skips the dead (`Player.aiStep` touches only while `getHealth() > 0`). `Unavailable` on a client.
- Not transactional: the pickup writes the inventory, the statistic, the ground entity and the experience outside the journal and sends its take packets and sounds at once, so an abort could not undo it (and restoring a journaled inventory would lose what it took). Inside an open transaction both calls return `Err(InTransaction)` and do nothing.
- Works for the host's own player (integrated server, LAN host) and for a LAN guest whose turn it is. On both, `pickup_xp` (NeoForge's `PlayerXpEvent.PickupXp`, fired inside `ExperienceOrb.playerTouch`) fires first and can veto the orb.
- `pickup_xp` now also fires on the host's own orb pass. Before, only guests' pickups fired it. The host's pass (`tick_experience`) now finds the orb on the lifted batch and takes it from the restored list through the new `host_take_orb`, which mirrors the guest's `guest_take_orb`: hook, `takeXpDelay = 2`, the take and sound, Mending, the points, then the count. A used-up orb now goes through `remove_orb`, so `entity_leave_level` hears it, as it already did for guests.
- The guest orb code is split out as `guest_take_orb`, and `as_guest_here` runs a closure in place when the guest's turn is already running. A resident pickup's Pre notice, insert, Post notice and statistic all go through it, so a guest's `touch_item` reaches them inside its own turn; a refused turn's Post keeps what `Inventory.add` left on the ground. Inside that turn the guest's `takeXpDelay` is `Game::xp_delay` (the turn swaps it in), so `guest_take_orb` sets it there, and the turn's end saves it.
