Changes are in e05854d394, in `crates/sunrise-client-gameplay/src/game/host/draw/gui_canvas.rs`.

1. When the visible tiles outnumber the GUI pixel cells of the clip meeting the posed rectangle, `fluid_tiles` (`gui_canvas.rs:362`) switches to `FluidTiles::Pixels` (`gui_canvas.rs:417`). The fill then emits one screen-space quad per GUI pixel cell whose centre is inside the rectangle, with the uv of the texel the tiling puts at that centre (`gui_canvas.rs:243`). Either way the quads are bounded by the clip's GUI pixels, and the repeat pattern is the one point sampling of the full tiling gives at those centres. The HUD atlas is packed, so a single quad with repeating UVs would sample neighbouring sprites, and I did not use one. Regression: `a_minified_fluid_rectangle_emits_at_most_one_quad_per_gui_pixel_of_the_clip` (`gui_canvas.rs:1785`). It uses your case at `Scale(1 / 256)` on 320 x 240, with the rectangle at (8, 8) so the phase is not zero. It asserts 76,800 quads, the first cells' extents, and that every uv is the tile's centre texel.
2. Each axis's first visible tile now comes from the tile phase at the clip edge (`tile_phase`, `gui_canvas.rs:334`, exact `rem_euclid` remainders; `gui_canvas.rs:405`), not from an index counted from `left`. Corners are posed in `f64` and narrowed to `f32` last (`gui_canvas.rs:215`). The test asserts the exact posed extents and UVs: `[-1e30, 0, 2e30, 16]` gives the screen's 20 whole tiles at x = 0, 16, ... (`gui_canvas.rs:1762`), and the 2^29 translated case gives four whole tiles at 0, 16, 32 and 48 (`gui_canvas.rs:1769`).

Follow-up: in `vanilla_water_and_lava_draw_their_block_still_sprites_as_an_items_fluid_layer_does`, lava's fixture sprite now has its own uv rectangle (`gui_canvas.rs:1615`), and each fluid's quad is checked against its own uv.
