1. No geometry is built in proportion to screen area any more. The per-pixel mode and the per-tile loop are both gone. `paint_fluid` (`crates/sunrise-client-gameplay/src/game/host/draw/gui_canvas.rs:182`) draws one `HudBatch::quad_tiled` (`gui_canvas.rs:214`), six vertices, over the part of the rectangle the clip can show (`fluid_fill`, `gui_canvas.rs:288`). That holds whatever the rectangle, the pose's scale or the screen. Regression: `a_minified_fluid_rectangle_on_a_4k_canvas_is_one_quad_of_six_vertices` (`gui_canvas.rs:1728`) uses your case: a 3840 x 2160 canvas at GUI scale 1, `Scale(1 / 256)`, a 1,000,000 px square from (8, 8). It asserts exactly six vertices with corners 1/32 .. (3840, 2160), repeats 0 .. (61,439.5, 34,559.5), the sprite's origin and extent, and a single `Blend::Tiled` run. A turned pose is also checked to stay one quad.
2. The minified fill is drawn through the shared GPU GUI path, and each vertex now carries its own coordinate. There the HUD atlas sampler did not repeat an atlas sub-rectangle, so I added the smallest support to that path, following its glint convention (special-purpose per-vertex fields, flat `light`):
   - `HudBatch::quad_tiled` (`crates/sunrise-client-render/src/hud/batch.rs:440`) writes each corner's place on the repeating grid to `uv`, the sprite's atlas origin to `glint_uv` and its atlas extent as two `f16` to `light`. The vertex layout is unchanged. `fs_tiled` decodes the halves with integer and `exp2` arithmetic (`half_bits`, `hud.wgsl:115`), as GLSL 330 has no `unpackHalf2x16`.
   - `Blend::Tiled` (`batch.rs:219`) and `AtlasBlend::Tiled` (`batch.rs:238`, for a sprite on a registered atlas page) draw with the new `HUD tiled sprite` pipeline (`hud/renderer.rs:339`, `:667`, `:682`).
   - That pipeline runs `fs_tiled` (`crates/sunrise-client-render/src/hud.wgsl:116`), which computes `origin + fract(place) * extent` and samples through the HUD's `atlas_sampler` at level 0 (`hud.wgsl:119`). Level 0 is the shared atlas's only level (`gfx/renderer_construction.rs:968`), so the wrap's jump at a repeat's edge never selects a mip.

   Each device pixel samples the texel the full tiling puts at its centre. The four vertices have distinct, interpolated places, so a minified sprite is no longer flattened to one texel per quad. `a_tiled_quad_carries_its_repeats_and_the_sprites_atlas_rect_in_six_vertices` (`hud/tests/batch.rs:329`) checks the vertex packing.
   - Round 3's phase and precision handling is kept in the new quad's corners and places: the phase is taken at the clip edge (`tile_phase`, `gui_canvas.rs:262`, used at `:319`), corners are posed in `f64` and narrowed last (`gui_canvas.rs:322`), and places are clamped finite (`:318`). `a_huge_or_far_off_fluid_rectangle_tiles_only_where_the_clip_shows_it` (`gui_canvas.rs:1660`) asserts the `[-1e30, 0, 2e30, 16]` and 2^29 cases as one quad with whole repeats (20 x 1 and 4 x 1).
