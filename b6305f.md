## What and why

Closes #6967.

Follow-up to #6305, which merged at 66724e0281 before its last review round (umceko's seven points; tracked in #6967). This PR lands that round on current main: the two regressions with no mods installed (the host's orb pass is again the pre-#6305 code word for word when no mod listens to `pickup_xp`, so pickup order, lookups and removal timing are vanilla's), and the five API points below, each with a test.

The rest of this description is the API as it stands after this PR.

For #6126 (closed by #6305): Nothing lets a mod make a player collect a given item entity or experience orb at once through the game's own pickup. Blood Magic's Sigil of Magnetism, like many magnet items, calls `itemEntity.playerTouch(player)` and `orb.playerTouch(player)` for each one in range. The Blood Magic port moves them onto the player and waits for the next player tick, one tick late. Copying the stack into the inventory by hand would skip the pickup hooks, the statistics, the animation and Mending.

What the PR adds, on `PlayerRef` (server side):
- `touch_item(host, item) -> Result<u32, PickupError>` (backend `player_touch_item`): `ItemEntity.playerTouch` now. It runs the game's pickup with its delay and target checks, `item_picked_up` and `item_picked_up_post`, `Inventory.add` (what does not fit stays on the ground), the statistic and the advancement criterion. `Ok(count)` is what `Inventory.add` took, whatever a Post listener then does with the rest; 0 when refused, and for an item whose own pickup is running (a call from its own `item_picked_up` / `item_picked_up_post` listener).
- `touch_orb(host, orb) -> Result<bool, PickupError>` (backend `player_touch_orb`): `ExperienceOrb.playerTouch` now. `Ok(false)` while `takeXpDelay` runs, on a `pickup_xp` veto, when the listener removed the orb, or when it started the player's delay by taking another orb. Otherwise the delay is set to 2, the orb's value goes through Mending and the rest to the player's experience, and one of its count is taken (a one-count orb is discarded). `Ok(true)` once the delay is set, even if a later listener removes the orb.
- Both send one take (`ClientboundTakeItemEntityPacket`) to every guest tracking the taken entity (`Entity.take` through `ServerChunkCache.broadcast`, `LanHub::take_item_tracked`), wherever the collector stands, for the host's own player and for a guest.
- New `PickupError { Stale, Unavailable, InTransaction, OtherLevel }`. `Stale` also for a dead player (the game's pickup pass skips the dead: `Player.aiStep` touches only while `getHealth() > 0`) and for an item or orb of another level than the one in place. `OtherLevel` for a player of another level (`Player.aiStep` touches its own level's entities only). `Unavailable` on a client.
- Not transactional: the pickup writes the inventory, the statistic, the ground entity and the experience outside the journal and sends its take packets and sounds at once, so an abort could not undo it (and restoring a journaled inventory would lose what it took). Inside an open transaction both calls return `Err(InTransaction)` and do nothing.
- `pickup_xp` (NeoForge's `PlayerXpEvent.PickupXp`, fired inside `ExperienceOrb.playerTouch`) now also fires for the host's own player, on its pass and on `touch_orb`. Before, only guests' pickups fired it.

How:
- With no `pickup_xp` listener, the host's `tick_experience` is main's code unchanged: the only change is an early branch taken while a mod hears `pickup_xp` (`host_hooked_orb_pass`).
- One orb take for the host's hooked pass, the guests' pass and `touch_orb`: `take_orb(OrbTaker, index, api)`, the guests' former sequence. The player-specific parts are at the boundary: whose `takeXpDelay`, the hook and Mending run in place for the host and in the guest's turn for a guest (`as_guest_here`), and `player_exp_cooldown_change` stays the guests' hook (their pass also fires it on each countdown tick; the host's does not). After a `pickup_xp` listener ran, the taker's delay and the orb are checked again.
- `as_guest_here` runs a closure in place when the guest's turn is already running, so a guest's `touch_item` reaches the resident pickup's Pre notice, insert, Post notice and statistic inside its own turn; a refused turn's Post keeps what `Inventory.add` left on the ground. Outside a guest's turn (the passes) it is `as_guest`.
- While a mod hears either item notice, the item's id is in `ServerState::touching_items` for the pickup sequence: a nested `touch_item` of it is `Ok(0)`.

No-mod tick cost: the host orb pass and the guest orb pass are main's code. The item pickup (host and guest) adds two `listened()` reads (one atomic load each) per item entity touched in a tick, and the host orb pass one `listened()` read per tick; no new lookup, allocation or removal runs without a listener. Not measured: below what a tick profile resolves.

Tests (all `#[ignore = "daily"]` in `sunrise-gameplay`, rows in `tools/ci/ignored-tests/daily-sunrise-client-player-touch.txt`; `native_item_entity_refs_server::tests` is `game::server_side::native_item_entity_refs_server::tests`):

A guest takes one orb, its `exp_cooldown` is 2, a second orb is refused (`Ok(false)`) and stays on the ground, and a touch of the taken orb is `Err(Stale)`.
- `the_host_player_touches_an_item_and_an_orb_through_the_game_pickup`: the same steps for the host's own player, plus its experience total (7, and still 7 after the refused orb).
- `a_pickup_xp_veto_refuses_a_touched_orb_for_the_host_and_for_a_guest`: a vetoing listener is called once for the host's `touch_orb` and once for a guest's. Both return `Ok(false)`, the orb keeps its count, no experience is given, and `takeXpDelay` stays 0.
- `game::lan_actions::pickup_xp_tests::pickup_xp_hears_and_can_veto_the_host_players_own_pickup`: the host's pass with no listener (main's code) and with a do-nothing listener (the hooked pass) give the same result (orb 7×2 → 7×1, 7 points, delay 2). A cancel keeps the orb whole with delay 0. A listener's `set_value(15)` is what the player gets. A listener's kill gives nothing and leaves the delay at 0.
- `a_post_listener_hears_a_partial_touch_and_the_rest_stays_on_the_ground`: with an `item_picked_up_post` listener, 8 apples offered and room for 5, for the host and for a guest: `Ok(5)`, the listener hears (player, 8, remainder 3), 3 stay on the ground, the slot holds 64. Then a target set to another player gives `Ok(0)`, an `item_picked_up` veto gives `Ok(0)`, and a creative player with a full inventory takes the rest (`Ok(3)`).
- `a_dead_player_or_an_open_transaction_refuses_a_touch`: a dead guest's `touch_item` and `touch_orb` are `Err(Stale)`; a living guest's inside a storage transaction are `Err(InTransaction)`; nothing is taken.
- `a_player_of_another_level_is_refused_a_touch`: a guest in the Nether while the Overworld is in place gets `Err(OtherLevel)` from both calls; the item and the orb stay.
- `a_touch_from_the_items_own_pickup_listener_takes_nothing`: guest 13's `touch_item` from the `item_picked_up` of guest 12's pickup of 8 is `Ok(0)`; guest 12 gets all 8, guest 13 nothing.
- `an_orb_taken_from_a_pickup_xp_listener_refuses_the_outer_pickup`: a `pickup_xp` listener takes a 3-point orb for the host's player during its touch of a 7-point orb: the inner touch is `Ok(true)`, the outer `Ok(false)`, the player has 3 points and the 7-point orb stays.
- `an_orb_removed_after_its_points_were_given_still_counts_as_taken`: a `player_xp_change` listener kills the orb after its points, for a guest (`player_xp_change` asks for the player whose turn it is): `touch_orb` is still `Ok(true)`, 7 points, delay 2.
- `touch_item_answers_what_the_inventory_took_whatever_a_post_listener_does`: 5 of 8 apples fit; a Post listener claiming all of it fit, and one discarding the item, both leave `Ok(5)` for the host's player, and the discard `Ok(5)` for a guest.

### Added in this follow-up (umceko's round on #6305)

- `PickupError::OtherLevel`: a player who is not in the level being ticked is refused a touch (a parked guest can't collect from another dimension).
- `Err(Stale)` for an orb found only in a parked level, matching items (it was a silent `Ok(false)`).
- `take_item_tracked` (`sunrise-sim/src/lan/containers.rs`, one line): the API calls send the take packet to every client tracking the taken entity.
- `ServerState::touching_items`: guards an item being picked up while a mod listens, so a nested `touch_item` of it from its own listener takes nothing (no duplicates).
