## What and why

Fixes #6123. A mod could not draw a fluid on a `GuiCanvas`: the canvas had `fill`, `item`, `atlas_sprite` and the rest, but nothing for a fluid, and no client read answers another mod's fluid sprite and tint, so mods guessed `block/<fluid>_still` with water's tint. The engine already resolves a fluid's still sprite and tint once per load for an item's fluid layer (`fluid_models::item_appearance`); the canvas had no way to use it.

This adds proposal 1 of the issue: `GuiCanvas::fluid(&mut self, fluid: &FluidStack, rect: [f32; 4]) -> Result<(), GuiDrawError>`.
- It draws the engine's one appearance of the fluid, the one an item's fluid layer and the world use (`fluid_models::item_appearance`): a registered fluid model's still sprite, else the look's sprite, else water's; the look's tint made opaque, white for a sprite with no tint, water's item colour 0xFF3F76E4 for a look with neither. Vanilla water is `block/water_still` times 0xFF3F76E4, lava `block/lava_still` untinted (`FluidContentsTint` answers -1 for lava).
- The sprite is read with `HudAtlas::texture_rect`, so a packed block-model sprite is found (the GUI lookup hides those); a registered atlas's sprite binds its page. A fluid no model knows draws the missing sprite untinted, as `FluidStateModelSet.get`'s `missingModel` does for an item; the empty fluid draws nothing.
- The sprite tiles at its own pixel size from the top left of `rect`, the last column and row cut, as JEI's `FluidTankRenderer.drawTiledSprite`. Each tile's corner comes from its integer index, so a far-off `rect` (where `x + 16.0` rounds back to `x`) ends after its tiles. Past 4096 tiles one sprite is stretched: Sunrise's own bound on what one call adds, since a mod's `rect` is any finite `f32` (JEI counts in `int` GUI pixels; vanilla has no GUI fluid draw).
- Uses the canvas pose and scissor like `fill`; corners are sorted; a non-finite rect gives `NotFinite`. A fluid that is not registered (an unresolved saved fluid) is refused with the new `GuiDrawError::UnknownFluid`.
- The stack's amount and components do not change the drawing; the engine has no tint provider that reads them yet (documented).

Not done: proposal 2, `ClientHost::fluid_look`. The issue says "either or both"; the canvas call covers AE2's terminal entries and fluid gauges, and a host read needs a new Wire type and dispatch. It can follow separately.

## Vanilla reference

Vanilla 26.3 has no GUI fluid draw of its own.
